abstract: "We study how vision-language models trained on Internet-scale data can be incorporated directly into end-to-end robotic control to boost generalization and enable emergent semantic reasoning. We propose to co-fine-tune state-of-the-art vision-language models on both robotic trajectory data and Internet-scale vision-language tasks. We express robotic actions as text tokens and incorporate them directly into the training set of the model in the same way as natural language tokens, creating vision-language-action models (VLA)."
