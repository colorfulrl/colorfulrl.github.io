abstract: "Traffic signal control is an important and challenging real-world problem, which aims to minimize the travel time of vehicles by coordinating their movements at the road intersections. Current traffic signal control systems in use still rely heavily on oversimplified information and rule-based methods, although we now have richer data, more computing power and advanced methods to drive the development of intelligent transportation. With the emerging trends of vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) communication technologies, vehicles can communicate with the traffic signals and the rich information from both vehicles and signals can be leveraged for better traffic signal control. In this survey, we focus on the recent advances of reinforcement learning approaches for traffic signal control. We classify the known approaches based on the settings of the problem and summarize the methods from the perspective of representative algorithms. We also introduce some traffic signal control benchmarks and discuss future directions."
