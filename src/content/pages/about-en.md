Hello, dear readers of my blog.

My name is Pham Nhat Khoa, currently a student at Da Nang University of Science and Technology, The University of Danang. I'm passionate about learning and researching Reinforcement Learning and World Models. This blog is where I share the knowledge, experience, and research I've gathered in this field. I hope this knowledge can reach people who are interested in or share the same passion as me. As you read through the posts, I'd love for you to leave feedback and suggestions so I can improve and refine this blog further.

Here's a quick overview of what this blog covers: posts focus mainly on foundational knowledge in Reinforcement Learning and World Models, verification of published research in this area, and small personal research projects I've done myself, backed by experimental results.
