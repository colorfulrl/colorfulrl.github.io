abstract: "Automatic Curriculum Learning (ACL) has become a cornerstone of recent successes in Deep Reinforcement Learning. These methods shape the learning trajectories of agents by challenging them with tasks adapted to their capacities. In this survey we review key ACL approaches and organize them by the mechanism used to select or generate tasks: self-play, goal generation, environment generation, and teacher-student frameworks. We discuss how these approaches relate to transfer learning, exploration, and multi-task RL."
