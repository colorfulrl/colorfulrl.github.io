abstract: "A wide range of reinforcement learning problems require specifying a distribution of tasks or environments in which a policy will be trained. However, creating a useful distribution of environments is error prone and takes significant developer effort. We propose PAIRED (Protagonist Antagonist Induced Regret Environment Design): an adversarial environment generator trained to maximize the regret of the protagonist agent, producing an automatic curriculum of environments at the frontier of the agent's capabilities. PAIRED yields zero-shot transfer to held-out environments far outside the training distribution."
