abstract: "Reinforcement learning is a learning paradigm for solving sequential decision-making problems. Recent years have witnessed remarkable progress in reinforcement learning upon the fast development of deep neural networks. However, the data-hungry nature of RL algorithms poses a critical obstacle to the practical deployment of such approaches, which typically require an unrealistic number of environment interactions. Transfer learning, which exploits previously acquired knowledge to accelerate learning in new tasks, has shown significant promise to mitigate this limitation. In this survey, we systematically investigate the recent progress of transfer learning approaches in the context of deep reinforcement learning."
