abstract: "The emerging field of quantum machine learning has the potential to substantially aid in the problems and scope of artificial intelligence. We present a general approach for the systematic treatment of machine learning, from the perspective of quantum information. We analyze how quantum effects can substantially speed up machine learning tasks based on reinforcement learning, including speedups that scale exponentially in the number of episodes of interaction with the environment. Our approach also naturally accommodates the fully quantum setting, where both agent and environment are quantum systems interacting coherently."
