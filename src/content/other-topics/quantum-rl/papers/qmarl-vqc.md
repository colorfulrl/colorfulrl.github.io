abstract: "In recent years, quantum computing has been getting a lot of attention from industry and academia. Among various QC research topics, variational quantum circuit (VQC) enables quantum deep reinforcement learning. Many studies of QRL have shown that QRL is superior to classical RL methods under constraints of the number of training parameters. We extend this to the multi-agent setting, proposing a centralized-training, decentralized-execution (CTDE) QMARL framework by designing novel VQCs for the actor and critic networks of each agent."
