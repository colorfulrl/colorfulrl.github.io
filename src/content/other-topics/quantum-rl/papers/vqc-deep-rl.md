abstract: "The state-of-the-art machine learning approaches are based on classical von Neumann computing architectures and have been widely used in many industrial and academic domains. With the recent development of quantum computing, researchers and tech-giants have attempted new quantum circuits for machine learning tasks. However, the existing quantum computing platforms are hard to simulate classical deep learning models or problems because of the intractability of deep quantum circuits. In this paper, we present the use of variational quantum circuits (VQC) in a hybrid quantum-classical framework for deep reinforcement learning. The proposed approach replaces the classical neural network with a VQC and demonstrates its capability to solve standard RL benchmarks with far fewer trainable parameters."
