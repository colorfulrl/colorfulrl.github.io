abstract: "Quantum machine learning has been identified as a promising field for near-term quantum devices, alongside optimization and quantum chemistry. Research has primarily focused on variational quantum algorithms, with proposals for enhancing supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning. Reinforcement learning remains the least studied area, with an open question about whether variational quantum algorithms can match classical neural network-based state-of-the-art algorithms on simple benchmark tasks. In this work, we demonstrate that data re-uploading and layerwise training enable VQC-based agents to match DQN performance on CartPole with orders of magnitude fewer parameters, without encountering barren plateau collapse."
