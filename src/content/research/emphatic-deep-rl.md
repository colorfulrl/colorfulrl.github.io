abstract: "Off-policy learning allows us to learn about possible policies of behavior from experience generated by a different behavior policy. Temporal-difference algorithms with function approximation can become unstable when combined with off-policy updates. Emphatic methods were designed to address this issue for linear function approximation. This paper extends them to deep RL agents. Directly applying ETD(λ) to deep RL algorithms that use forward-view multi-step returns performs poorly; the authors derive new emphatic algorithms for that setting, and show gains on small problems designed to expose TD instability and on Atari games from the Arcade Learning Environment."
