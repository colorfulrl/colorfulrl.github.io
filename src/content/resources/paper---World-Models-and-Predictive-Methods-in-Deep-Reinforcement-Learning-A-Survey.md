description: "This paper investigates and develops techniques and theory for implementing state-of-the-art model-based reinforcement learning techniques. First, neuroscientific ideas of prediction and inference are developed. The basics of deep learning and the reinforcement learning problem are then discussed. Deep model-free and model-based methods are developed in the context of later discussions. Theory of latent representation and predictive methods is presented before discussing advanced state-of-the-art models. Emphasis is placed on how these agents apply a broad range of the previously developed techniques. A discussion and implementation of a state-of-the-art agent (MuZero) in Python follows. Finally, the direction of the field as a whole is discussed."
