title: "Collaborative Scheduling for Electromagnetic Detection Satellites: Model and Reinforcement Learning-based Evolutionary Approach"
author: "Yanjie Song, Yue Zhang, Yonghao Du, Lining Xing, Witold Pedrycz"
description: "Nghiên cứu các mô hình và thuật toán lập lịch cộng tác cho vệ tinh trinh sát điện từ, sử dụng framework kết hợp học tăng cường và thuật toán tiến hóa (LBEA)."
descriptionEn: "Detection satellites form a class of satellites that have the ability to detect and obtain corresponding data on all kinds of stationary or moving objects in the sea, on land, and in the air using their payloads. An electromagnetic detection satellite (EDS) uses receiver and antenna to complete the signal processing and transmission.  Based on the analysis of related literature and the difficulties and challenges faced by collaborative scheduling, this book focuses on the models and algorithms related to EDS scheduling problems. The basic model of collaborative scheduling is constructed. The LBEA framework is also established. The methods adopted are also of good reference significance for the resource collaborative scheduling and optimization problems of other resource control systems. The main content is divided into 5 parts. The 1st part is an overview, which provides the background and the current state of development. The 2nd part is the basic model of cooperative scheduling and the framework of learning evolutionary algorithms. The 3rd part examines cooperative scheduling of homogeneous EDSs for stationary target detection. The 4th part reviews research on cooperative planning of heterogeneous EDSs for low-speed moving target detection. The 5th and final part examines cooperative scheduling of heterogeneous EDSs for high-speed moving target detection."
