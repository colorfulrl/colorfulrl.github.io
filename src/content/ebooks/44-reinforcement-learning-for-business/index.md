For each example, you’ll step into the role of a consultant, analyzing how a problem can be effectively solved with RL. You’ll discover full coverage of the latest and most relevant techniques for RL, including utilizing reinforcement learning with human feedback (RLHF) to align large language models into business objectives and constraints."
